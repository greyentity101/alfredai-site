Research Focus

Where the work sits

An honest, jargon-light map of the problems I care about.

I operate at the intersection of applied AI and clinical imaging infrastructure: building model-agnostic tools that let radiology departments evaluate, deploy, and govern AI safely — while pushing the boundaries of detector physics (photon-counting CT) and spectral imaging informatics.

  • Photon-counting CT & detector physics — CdTe/CZT and deep-silicon readout ASICs, vendor landscape (Siemens NAEOTOM Alpha, GE Photon Spectra, Canon Ultimion), and spectral data handling at the PACS reading console.

  • DICOM-native AI orchestration — STOW-RS ingestion, model-agnostic worker routing, structured reporting (DICOM SR, FHIR R4), and end-to-end evaluation of latency, segmentation accuracy, and interoperability.

  • Clinical validation & benchmarking — E1–E4 evaluation protocol measuring p95 latency, Dice/HD95 segmentation, finding-level grounding fidelity, and round-trip integrity on commodity hardware.
